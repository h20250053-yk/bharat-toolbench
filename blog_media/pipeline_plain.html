How one request is tested

1 · The request
The same task, asked in English, Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi or Gujarati
↓
2 · The AI model
reads the request and the list of six tools, and picks a tool
3 · The pretend railway
runs the tool and sends back the result
↔ back and forth, up to 8 turns, until the model gives its final answer
↓
4 · The automatic checker (no AI judge)
✓ the right bookings, and no extra ones
✓ a correct summary
✓ the right tools, with the right details, in a sensible order
↓
5 · The result
success or failure, plus a label saying what went wrong
