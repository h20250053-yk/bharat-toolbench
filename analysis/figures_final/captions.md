# Figure captions (generated from the run files by analysis/build_results.py)

All charts: PNG at 2x size and PDF; the same colour identifies the same model in every chart; failure-type colours are separate.

**fig1_overall_success** (fig1_overall_success.png / fig1_overall_success.pdf): Gemma-4-26B-A4B is the best model at 76.3% task success and Qwen2.5-3B the weakest at 3.5%; the format layer matters most for Llama-3.2-3B (lenient 7.5% vs strict 4.0%). Error bars: 95% Wilson intervals over the 375 items; the dashed line is a model that never calls a tool and always asks for clarification (12.0%); 5 of 8 models score below it (4 of 7 excluding the unreliable model(s) marked with a dagger: Mistral-Small-3.2-24B †: unreliable, 229/375 replies discarded by Ollama's parser (serving bug), excluded from ranking and size/family claims).

**fig2_model_language_heatmap** (fig2_model_language_heatmap.png / fig2_model_language_heatmap.pdf): The spread between models (72.8 percentage points between the best and the weakest model, averaged over languages) is larger than the spread between languages (6.7 points between the easiest, English 56.0%, and the hardest, Marathi 49.3%, averaged over the three models well above the floor: Qwen2.5-14B, gpt-oss-20B, Gemma-4-26B-A4B). For models at or below the null baseline the language comparison is not interpretable (floor effect). Each cell is 75 items, so single cells carry wide intervals (see results_final/by_language.csv).

**fig11_language_focus** (fig11_language_focus.png / fig11_language_focus.pdf): Per-language task success for Qwen2.5-14B, gpt-oss-20B, Gemma-4-26B-A4B, the models well above the 0% floor (the only ones for which a language comparison is interpretable); 2 of 12 of their gaps to English exclude zero in the 95% paired bootstrap (Qwen2.5-14B Marathi -13.3; Qwen2.5-14B Gujarati -13.3 points). Error bars: 95% Wilson intervals over 75 items.

**fig3_score_vs_size** (fig3_score_vs_size.png / fig3_score_vs_size.pdf): Within families: Qwen 3.5% (3B) -> 26.9% (14B), higher with size; Llama 7.5% (3B) -> 11.2% (8B), higher with size. Across the 7 reliable models (the unreliable one is drawn hollow and left out) Spearman rho between size and success = 0.89 (p = 0.007; indicative only).
